Model 
x = L₁·cos(θ₁) + L₂·cos(θ₁ + θ₂)
y = L₁·sin(θ₁) + L₂·sin(θ₁ + θ₂)

cos(θ₂) = (x² + y² − L₁² − L₂²) / (2·L₁·L₂)
θ₁ = atan2(y, x) − atan2(L₂·sin θ₂, L₁ + L₂·cos θ₂)

Angles are counterclockwise from +x. θ₂ < 0 puts the elbow above the base-to-target line (elbow up); θ₂ > 0 puts it below. If the target is out of reach, the arm shows the closest pose and the error is the remaining distance.
